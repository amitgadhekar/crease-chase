How to bat
  • Pull back like a slingshot: put your finger down and drag away from where you want the ball to go. Drag down to hit it straight past the bowler, down-left for a cover drive, left for a cut, right for a pull, up for fine shots behind you.
  • Shots stay on the ground by default. Tap Loft before you let go to hit that ball in the air; it switches off after every ball. A longer pull hits harder, up to the dashed ring.
  • Let go to swing, just as the ball reaches the bat. The bar at the bottom shows how early or late you were.
  • Tap without dragging to block. Don't touch the screen to leave the ball.
  • Move your feet with the stick at the bottom left (or the arrow keys) until the ball pitches: down the pitch to turn a good-length ball into a half-volley, back to give yourself time against short ones. Miss it out of your crease and you can be stumped; miss it with your pads in front of the stumps and you can be out LBW.
  • Early swings go squarer to the leg side, late ones to the off side. Drive full balls, cut and pull short ones.
